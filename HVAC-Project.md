## Understanding the CYK-24

The CYK-24 is Pioneer's **slim one-way ceiling cassette platform** — NOT a packaged deal.

- Slim rectangular body: ~50.25" L × 13.25" W × 9" H
- Depth under 14" — **fits between 16" on-center joists**
- Built-in condensate lift pump (~30" vertical lift)
- One-way airflow (single direction, not 4-way square)

**Two versions exist — order the correct one:**

| Version | Name | Fits Joists? | Includes Outdoor Unit? |
|---|---|---|---|
| Single-zone full set | CYK009GMFILCAD | Yes | Yes (single-zone only) |
| **Multi-zone indoor section** | Quantum Series One-Way 9K | **Yes** | **No — pairs with your outdoor unit** |

**Order the multi-zone indoor section only.**

**Do NOT order:** Pioneer Quantum Ultra ceiling cassette (CT009GLSILCFHG) — that is a square 22.5"×22.5" 8-way unit that does NOT fit between joists.

## Line Sets (Pre-Made — No Flaring Required)

Order factory pre-flared line sets to eliminate the flaring step entirely.

**Standard lengths available:** 16 ft, 25 ft, 33 ft, 50 ft

**Per zone sizes:**
- Liquid line: 1/4" OD copper
- Suction line: 1/2" OD copper

**Measuring instructions:**
1. Measure from each indoor unit location, through wall penetration, down to outdoor unit
2. Add 2–3 ft for routing curves and slack
3. Order the next standard length up
4. Up to ~3 ft of excess can be coiled near outdoor unit — acceptable

**If any run exceeds 25 ft:** HVAC tech must add refrigerant (requires EPA 608)

**Pioneer Home Depot bundle** (includes 3 × 16 ft line sets):
Model: `WYT030GLSI24M3-9W16-9W16-9W16`

## Condensate Drain Lines

### CYK-24 Ceiling Cassette (Room 3)
- Built-in condensate pump — 30" vertical lift
- 3/4" drain line exits unit
- After pump outlet: slope 1/4" per foot continuously toward drain
- No traps or low spots after the pump outlet
- Route options: through rim joist to outside, floor drain, utility sink drain

### RT009 Concealed Duct (Rooms 1 & 2)
- Also have built-in condensate pumps (~25" lift)
- Same 3/4" drain line, same slope rules
- Plan the route **before** closing up the ceiling cavity
- Insulate drain line in unconditioned spaces

### DO NOT tap into plumbing vent stack
- Vent portion (above fixtures) carries air/gases only
- Tapping in creates sewer gas backflow into HVAC units
- Route outside through rim joist is cleanest, code-compliant solution

## Key Technical Notes

- **Vacuum target:** 500 microns or below — use a micron gauge, not just manifold gauges
- **Flare nut torque:** 1/4" line = 14–18 ft-lbs / 1/2" line = 50–60 ft-lbs
- **Refrigerant:** R-454B (new low-GWP formula — EPA 608 required to handle)
- **Pre-charge covers:** up to 25 ft per circuit from factory
- **Indoor unit mix:** wall + concealed duct + ceiling cassette all supported on same outdoor unit
- **Load rule:** total indoor BTU can be 66%–133% of outdoor unit rated capacity
- **Your indoor total:** 27,000 BTU on 27,000 BTU outdoor = 100% — perfect

### Questions to Ask Any Seller Before Ordering

1. **Do your ceiling cassette indoor units fit between 16" on-center joists?** (Need width < 14.5" and depth < 14")
2. **Do the cassette indoor units have a built-in condensate lift pump?** (If not, you need gravity drain which requires sloped ceiling)
3. **Does this system come pre-charged, or do I need an HVAC tech to add refrigerant?**
4. **What are the pre-charge line lengths per circuit?** (Need to cover your longest run)
5. **Are the indoor units from a brand-matched system or third-party?** (Mixed brands can void warranty)

### Should You Care About -22°F vs -13°F?

This depends entirely on your local winter lows. Pioneer heats down to **-13°F**:

- If your area never sees below -13°F: the Thermospace HyperHeat advantage is irrelevant
- If temps regularly hit -15°F to -22°F: Thermospace keeps running at full capacity; Pioneer may derate or shut down

**Check your local record low** (NOAA historical records for your zip code). For most of the continental US outside the northern Great Plains and mountain states, -13°F is sufficient.

If you're in a climate where -22°F matters, note that **ACiQ Extreme Slim Cassette** also reaches -22°F, uses R-454B (not R-410A), fits 16" OC joists, and costs roughly the same as Pioneer. ACiQ becomes the better HyperHeat alternative.

---

### R-410A Warning (Thermospace)

R-410A is being phased out under the AIM Act and EPA Section 608 refrigerant regulations. As of January 1, 2025:
- New equipment using R-410A cannot be manufactured or imported (commercial HVAC)
- Residential timeline follows shortly — R-410A supply will tighten, prices will rise
- Service calls requiring refrigerant addition will become more expensive over time
- R-454B (Pioneer) and R-32 are the replacement standards

Installing an R-410A system today means a shorter useful service life before refrigerant becomes a maintenance cost problem. For a long-term installation, R-454B is strongly preferred.
